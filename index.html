How to order

  1. Sign in and add

    Tap Add on anything you like. The first time, sign in with Google. Your basket is saved to your account.

  2. Place your order

    Open your basket, add a note per item if you like, and tap Place order. First order? Enter your name, mobile and address once.

  3. Pick your delivery

    We add Normal and Fast delivery charges to your order. Choose one in Your orders and accept it. That confirms your order. Delivery charges are non-refundable.

  4. We make it by hand

    Once your order is confirmed we start crocheting. Track it anytime in Your orders.

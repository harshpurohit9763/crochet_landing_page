How to order

  1. Sign in and add

    Tap Add on anything you like. The first time, sign in with Google. Your basket is saved to your account.

  2. Place your order

    Open your basket, add a note for colour or size, and tap Order on WhatsApp. First order? Enter your name, mobile and address once.

  3. Send it on WhatsApp

    Your order is saved with a code like FG-1A2B3C. Tap Send on WhatsApp so the message reaches us.

  4. We confirm and make it

    We add the delivery charge, confirm the total, then make it by hand. Track it anytime in Your orders.
